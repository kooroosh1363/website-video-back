01 / Delivery

Progressive media instead of unconditional autoplay.

Motion policy

Respect user preferences

Reduced-motion users stay on the poster/fallback layer and never need the looping video.

Network policy

Skip video on constrained connections

Save-Data and very slow effective connection types prevent the optional video request.

Control

Pause is always available

The background is decorative, muted, and paired with an explicit accessible play/pause control.

02 / Engineering

The fallback is the product too.

The page is fully readable before video loads. The poster and CSS treatment carry the composition, so motion enhances the experience rather than becoming a dependency.

  • semantic navigation and real anchors
  • no external font request
  • no autoplay when reduced motion is requested
  • no video request when Save-Data is active
  • testable media-loading policy
  • asset-budget checks in CI
